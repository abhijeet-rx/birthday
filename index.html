A Special Message For You

Hey Pihu,

I don't think I've ever really known how to put what I feel for you into words. Sometimes I feel like love is such a small word for something that has become such a huge part of my life.

I didn't fall in love with you because of the way you look, or because of some perfect version of you that exists in my head. I fell in love with you. The person you are, the way you think, the way you feel, the little things you do without even realizing them, your imperfections, your mistakes, your moods, your softness, your chaos — all of it.

And maybe that's what I love most about us. I don't want only the easy parts of you. I want all of you. I want to know you when you're happy, when you're tired, when you're confused, when you don't feel like talking, and even when you don't know how to explain what's going on inside you. I want to be the person who stays beside you in the dark until your light comes back.

Somewhere along the way, you became the best part of my days. And suddenly, all those love songs started making sense because somehow, every love song started sounding like you.

There are so many things about you that I could try to explain, but some things are better felt than explained. There's this connection between us that I don't think I'll ever completely understand. It's like you speak a language I didn't even know I knew until I met you.

And I want you to know something — I choose you. Not only when everything is perfect. I choose you despite the distance, despite the difficult days, despite the moments when things aren't easy. Because loving you isn't something I want to do only when it's convenient. I want to love you anyway. I want to love you even though.

Maybe there will be days when the distance feels longer, when life gets complicated, or when we don't know exactly what comes next. But if the destination is you, I don't think distance or time can scare me that much.

I don't want to make some dramatic promise that everything will always be perfect. I can't promise that. But I can promise you that what I feel for you is real. And as long as I exist, you'll be loved.

I think that's what you have become to me — not just someone I love, but someone who has quietly found a place inside my existence. My heart feels so full of you that sometimes it hardly feels like it's mine anymore.

And if someday you forget how much you mean to me, I hope you remember this:

I would still choose you.

In every version of us.
On the easy days.
On the difficult ones.
Across every distance.
And in every lifetime where I somehow get the chance to find you again.

Because somewhere between meeting you and becoming us, I realized something I don't think I'll ever be able to unfeel:

I didn't just fall in love with you.

I found a part of myself in loving you.

Avhiii 💖
